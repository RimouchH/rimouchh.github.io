Computational Social Scientist · Communication Scholar

I am a computational social scientist and communication scholar studying how artificial intelligence and digital media shape persuasion, representation, consumer and audience responses, and health communication. I use large-scale data, machine learning, and experimental methods alongside qualitative analysis to study communication across digital platforms and cultural contexts.
