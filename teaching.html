Teaching philosophy

I teach communication as both a craft and a way of understanding the world. My classroom connects theory to the platforms, data, and cultural moments students already live in, helping them become sharp analysts, ethical communicators, and confident creators. Whether teaching public relations writing, media literacy, or the role of AI in communication, I aim to leave students not just with skills, but with the curiosity to keep asking better questions.
