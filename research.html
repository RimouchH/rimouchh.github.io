Research

Research program

As a computational social scientist, I study how artificial intelligence and digital media reshape persuasion, representation, and audience response, spanning advertising and public relations, health communication, and platform society. I combine large-scale computational analysis with experimental and interpretive methods to ask social questions at the scale digital platforms now operate.

AI & Emerging Media

Artificial intelligence, persuasion, and audience response

How audiences and consumers respond to AI-generated versus human-generated content, virtual influencers, and algorithmically mediated communication, and what these shifts mean for persuasion, authenticity, and trust.

Advertising & Public Relations

Branding, representation, and strategic communication

Consumer reactions to rainbow-washing and performative advertising, diversity and representation in virtual influencer campaigns, disability and gender representation, and strategic communication across digital and traditional platforms.

Digital Health Communication

Online health communities and information integrity

Reproductive health, contraceptive risk negotiation, health misinformation, and social support in pseudonymous online communities, including how for-profit actors disguise persuasion as peer support.

Platforms & Society

Culture, gender, and cross-cultural media

Cross-cultural media framing, feminist digital activism, gendered narratives, and information integrity across Reddit, Instagram, and TikTok, with attention to marginalized communities and the Global South.

Methodological Contribution

The Iterative Bidirectional Validation (IBV) framework

A human-in-the-loop approach to validating large language model classifications in sensitive communication contexts, developed to bring rigor and transparency to computational text analysis in health and social domains.

Methods

  • Experimental & survey design
  • Machine learning classification
  • Natural language processing
  • Large language models
  • Topic modeling (BERTopic, LDA)
  • Emotion & sentiment analysis
  • Multimodal analysis
  • Large-scale social media data
