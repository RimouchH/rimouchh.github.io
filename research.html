Research

Research program

As a computational social scientist and strategic communication scholar, I study how media, digital platforms, and emerging technologies shape attitudes, behaviors, identities, and decision-making, with particular attention to health, representation, and social issues. I combine media effects, persuasion, strategic communication, and computational social science, using large-scale data alongside experiments and mixed-method approaches.

AI & Emerging Media

AI-generated communication and human-AI interaction

AI-generated communication, large language models, human-AI interaction, information integrity, and virtual influencers, and what these shifts mean for persuasion, authenticity, and trust.

Strategic Communication & Media Effects

Persuasion, branding, and audience effects

Public relations, advertising, persuasion, branding, campaigns, and audience effects, including consumer responses to performative advertising and representation in strategic communication.

Digital Health Communication

Online health communities and information seeking

Reproductive health, online health communities, health misinformation, information seeking, and social support, including how for-profit actors disguise persuasion as peer support.

Identity, Representation & Digital Culture

Gender, representation, and digital culture

Gender, representation, digital activism, LGBTQIA+ communities, cross-cultural communication, and online identity, with attention to marginalized communities and the Global South.

Methodological Contribution

The Iterative Bidirectional Validation (IBV) framework

A human-in-the-loop approach to validating large language model classifications in sensitive communication contexts, developed to bring rigor and transparency to computational text analysis in health and social domains.

Methods

  • Transformer models
  • Natural language processing
  • Machine learning
  • Large language models
  • Sentiment & emotion analysis
  • Multimodal analysis
  • Experimental & survey design
  • Large-scale social media data
